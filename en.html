A word from the founder
“The body carries our emotions, exhaustion and the pace of everyday life. I founded SAGE & SOMA to create space for thoughtful therapeutic touch — not simply a pause for tired muscles, but a sense of safety and renewal.”

We opened SAGE & SOMA in response to the exhaustion of urban life. Every detail — from soft olive and cream finishes to the scent of wild mountain sage — invites you to slow down.

Our concept brings together medical and osteopathic perspectives, deep-tissue myofascial release, facial sculpting, craniosacral techniques and botanical extracts.

Your visit

Frequently asked questions

How should I prepare for a massage?

+
We recommend avoiding a heavy meal for around 90 minutes before your session and staying hydrated. Your visit begins with herbal tea and a conversation about your preferences.

Which oils do you use, and what if my skin is sensitive?

+
Our concept uses organic cold-pressed base oils such as jojoba, sweet almond and grapeseed, paired with botanical essential oils. Please discuss sensitivities and allergies with your therapist before treatment.

Can I arrange a gift certificate?

+
The concept includes both cotton-paper gift certificates in sage envelopes and digital certificates that can be sent as a gift.
